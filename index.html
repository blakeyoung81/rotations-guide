Rotations Guide

A clerkship companion for third-year medical students: contacts, Day 1 plans, sites, schedules and student advice in one place.

Support

Questions, corrections to rotation info, or bugs: blakeyoung81@gmail.com.

Rotations Guide is built by a student, for students. It is not an official publication of any school or hospital. Always follow your clerkship's official instructions when they differ from the app.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Signing in. You sign in with your school email and a one-time 6-digit code we email to you. We keep your email address to identify your posts and preferences. Codes are stored hashed and expire quickly.

What you choose to post. Advice, notes, comments, events, tips about attendings, and lunch-board posts you write are visible to other signed-in students. Attending tips can be posted anonymously. You can delete your own posts.

Your schedule. Your block schedule is stored on your phone. If you allow calendar access, it is copied into a "Rotations Guide" calendar on your phone.

Location. Location is used on your phone to show nearby sites, give directions and check you in at a site. Your GPS coordinates are never sent to us. Sharing is off by default and controlled by you:

  • Share my check-ins publishes the site, rotation and time you checked in (never coordinates) to classmates and program staff. Check-ins expire after 12 hours.
  • Show my schedule to classmates lists you at the site your schedule places you.
  • Notify me when classmates arrive shows a banner while you're checked in.

Contacts. If you allow it, the app saves preceptor and site details into your Contacts. It does not read or upload your contacts.

Program staff. Clerkship staff with an access code can see who is checked in or scheduled at each site, using only what students have chosen to share.

What we don't do. No ads, no selling data, no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data. Email blakeyoung81@gmail.com from your school address and we'll delete your account data and posts.
